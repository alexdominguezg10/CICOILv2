for deploying CICOILv2.0 (OpenDrift 1.14.9 + CICOIL extensions + photooxidation)
to the CHAMAN2 HPC system.

Photooxidation Process (NEW)
----------------------------

Aromatic groups G1–G8 photooxidized by solar UV when at surface.

Rate: dM_i = k_i * E_UV_eff * M_i (kg/timestep)
  k_i: Per-group rate constant (m² J⁻¹)
       G1 (benzene)  = 2.9e-8  → t½ ~14 d
       G2 (toluene)  = 2.0e-8  → t½ ~20 d
       ...
       G8 (pyrene+)  = 5.0e-6  → t½ ~hours

  E_UV_eff = I_UV [W/m²] * Δt [s] * f_surface

  I_UV: Synthetic diurnal (cosine solar-zenith model, peak 50 W/m²)
  f_surface: Delvigne & Sweeney (1988) wave-submergence correction
